Three squads, three different questions

There is no optimal squad, only an optimal squad given an objective. Each draft below answers one question honestly and accepts what that costs it elsewhere. Every pick is re-derived from the live FPL API: availability, budget, club limits and the position-dependent defensive-contribution threshold.

What is checked, and what is not

Verified against the API

  • 15 players: 2 GKP, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD
  • Maximum 3 players per club
  • Total cost within £100.0m
  • Every player status = a and no reduced chance of playing
  • Legal XI: exactly 1 GKP, at least 3 DEF, at least 1 FWD
  • Defensive contribution per 90 against the real threshold

Deliberately not claimed

  • No projected point totals. Zero of 38 gameweeks have been played, so last season's rates are a prior, not a forecast.
  • No probability of returns. FPL's per-match history resets at the season boundary and the endpoint is not available to us.
  • A summer signing's record was earned at his previous club. Those players are marked, not silently re-attributed.

The DefCon rule these drafts are built on

Defensive contribution pays +2 points when a player passes a threshold in a single match. The threshold, and the actions that count, depend on position:

PositionCounting actionsBar per match
DEFclearances + blocks + interceptions + tackles10
MID / FWDthe same, plus ball recoveries12

Recoveries pay a midfielder and pay a defender nothing. Verified against the API: Senesi 357 + 62 = 419, matching his recorded total exactly, while Anderson needs his 306 recoveries to reach 515 — without them he sits at 209. Six of every ten actions Anderson makes are in a category a defender is not paid for. That is why the bar is lower at the back.

The bonus is capped at +2 per match. Twenty actions pays exactly what twelve pays, so volume above the bar is wasted. It is a threshold, never a tally.
