What is measured and what is not

Prior — not measured
Who starts. This is a current external projection, not a fact. It can be wrong, and on several clubs the measured record shows why it deserves doubt.
Measured — 2025/26 record
Price, ownership, minutes, starts, total points and points per game are measured fields from the completed 2025/26 season.
Derived — auditable
Each block is that player's season total for one scoring category, scaled by points per game ÷ total points. Both factors are measured fields, so no appearance count is inferred, and the blocks sum to the measured points-per-game figure. Rounding to two decimals can leave the sum a hundredth short.
Why points per game
Per-90 divides by minutes played, which quietly deletes the games a manager got nothing from: a substitute who plays twenty minutes a week can outrank a regular starter. Points per game played keeps those games in. Appearance points cannot exceed 2 per match, because a match pays at most 2 for turning up — a handful of players show 2.01–2.06 here, which is FPL rounding its published points-per-game to one decimal, not a player beating the ceiling.
No composite score
There is no 0–100 rating anywhere on this page. A hand-weighted index is an editorial opinion wearing a number's clothes, and it fails without saying so. The breakdown is published instead so a reader can disagree with the conclusion without doubting the data.
